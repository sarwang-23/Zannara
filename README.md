# Zanarra — One Universe. Infinite Stories.

A complete, production-grade, responsive brand platform for **Zanarra**, an AI-native children's entertainment and intellectual property company building original characters, immersive story universes, and globally scalable children's franchises from India for the world.

---

## 🌟 Brand Foundation & Philosophy

- **Brand Name**: Zanarra
- **Tagline**: *"Small questions. Big adventures."*
- **Franchise Vision**: *"One universe. Infinite stories."*
- **Positioning**: A global children's IP company from India, using AI to create, localise and scale original characters, stories and entertainment experiences.
- **Brand Promise**: *"We create characters children love, worlds they want to explore, and stories that grow with them."*
- **Design Aesthetic**: Premium warm ivory (`#FAF7F0`), soft cream (`#F2EBDD`), deep midnight navy (`#17233A`), and muted gold (`#C7A451`) palette. Light, editorial, and cinematic without generic AI gradients or dark deck aesthetics.

## 🚀 Pages & Features Built

1. **Homepage (`/`)**:
   - **Section 1: Cinematic Hero** — Original vector illustration featuring **Momo, Tobo, and Dodi** together in a magical setting.
   - **Section 2: Brand Idea** — Wide editorial layout on *"One universe. Infinite stories."*
   - **Section 3: Meet the Characters** — Feature panels for Momo (The Leader), Tobo (The Clumsy Genius), and Dodi (The Tiny Spark).
   - **Section 4: Explore the Five Realms** — Cohesive tiles for *Terra Viva*, *Mechanika*, *Mythara*, *Sonika*, and *Cosmara*.
   - **Section 5: More than Entertainment** — 4 editorial blocks: Curiosity, Creativity, Connection, and Discovery.
   - **Section 6: One Universe, Many Experiences** — 8-step visual roadmap from short stories to global licensing.
   - **Section 7: Built for a Global Audience** — Dedicated map graphic spotlighting India to international resonance.
   - **Section 8: For Partners & Investors** — Dual prominent CTA portals.
   - **Section 9: Final Wonder CTA**.

2. **The Zanarra Universe (`/universe`)**:
   - Narrative architecture (Characters, Realms, Stories, Products).
   - **Interactive Universe Map**: Clickable cartography nodes linking the 5 realms with dynamic detail drawers.
   - *"One universe, many journeys"* modular storytelling principles.

3. **Characters Directory (`/characters`)**:
   - Interactive trait filters: *All Characters*, *Adventurers*, *Inventors*, *Curious Minds*.
   - Detailed profiles: Ages, strengths, funny quirks, why children relate, signature premises, and realm associations.
   - Synergy section: *"Three friends. Three different ways of seeing the world."*

4. **Stories & Experiences (`/stories`)**:
   - Category filtering: *Short Stories*, *Songs & Music*, *Adventures*, *Discover & Learn*, *Behind the Worlds*.
   - Honest pipeline badges (*Pilot Stage*, *Concept Arc*, *In Development*, *Coming Soon*).
   - Early access parent/partner circle form with COPPA-compliant child privacy protection.

5. **Our Business (`/business`)**:
   - Three-stage engine (*Stage 1: Create*, *Stage 2: Build*, *Stage 3: Monetise & Expand*).
   - **Compounding IP Flywheel**: Interactive cyclical node explorer (`Original IP → Content → Audience → Affinity → Products → Revenue → More IP`).
   - Breakdown of the 7 planned revenue streams.

6. **Investors (`/investors`)**:
   - Investment Thesis & 5 thesis cards.
   - **Interactive 5-Year Financial Chart (FY27–FY31)**: ₹0.75 Cr to ₹110 Cr with EBITDA milestone (FY29) and target PAT (₹29.5 Cr).
   - Commercial logic & roadmap (5 phases to ₹100–120 Cr).
   - Long-term ambition towards ₹1,000 Cr+ valuation with assumptions disclosures.
   - Confidential Investor Information request modal.

7. **Partnerships & Licensing (`/partnerships`)**:
   - 8 active licensing & collaboration pathways.
   - Fully validated partnership enquiry form.

8. **About Zanarra (`/about`)**:
   - Ambition narrative, 5 core guiding values, and creative integrity standards.

9. **Contact & Pathways (`/contact`)**:
   - 5 dedicated routing pathways (*General*, *Investors*, *Licensing*, *Press*, *Careers*).
   - Form with input validation and feedback states.
